# Request: Upper Wawa Dam telemetry

**To:** Office of Civil Defense CALABARZON (Region IV-A)
**Cc:** Manila Water (Upper Wawa operator)
**Subject:** Request for machine-readable Upper Wawa Dam data

---

Good day,

I am building a free, non-commercial flood information app for residents
downstream of Upper Wawa Dam — Rodriguez, San Mateo and Marikina.

Your bulletins are the **only public source** of Upper Wawa water level,
spilling status and spill rate that I have been able to find. PAGASA publishes
Marikina river gauges through a web system I can read automatically, but nothing
equivalent exists for Upper Wawa, which sits at the top of that same watershed
and is the earliest warning the whole chain gets.

At present the only way to read your bulletins is from your Facebook page. That
is fragile for something people may depend on during a typhoon, and I would
rather not build on it.

**What would help, in order of preference:**

1. A data feed or API for Upper Wawa level, spilling status and spill rate
2. A web page publishing the same values in a consistent format
3. Permission and a supported method to read your published bulletins
   programmatically

Even a plain page updated on the same schedule as the bulletins would be a large
improvement over the current situation.

**What the app does with it:** it tells a resident when Upper Wawa starts
spilling or the spill rate rises sharply, and that they are downstream. It
carries a prominent notice that it is not an official warning service, tells
people to follow OCD and their LGU regardless of what it shows, never displays a
lower alert level than the official one, and attributes every reading to your
office with a link. It is free, has no advertising and no user accounts.

I am happy to work within whatever conditions you would want to set — rate
limits, attribution, a usage agreement, or a review of how the data is
presented.

Thank you for the work your office does publishing these bulletins during
storms.
