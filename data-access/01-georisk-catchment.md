# Request: Marikina River catchment boundary

**To:** UP Project NOAH / GeoRiskPH (DOST-PHIVOLCS)
**Subject:** Request for Marikina River catchment boundary (GeoJSON or shapefile)

---

Good day,

I am building a free, non-commercial flood information app for residents of the
Marikina river corridor. It reads published PAGASA river gauge data and tells a
resident whether a change upstream affects their specific location.

To do that correctly I need an authoritative **catchment boundary for the
Marikina River basin** — ideally as GeoJSON or shapefile in WGS84.

I want to be direct about why I am asking rather than approximating it myself. I
can draw a plausible boundary, but a wrong boundary means telling somebody
downstream that they are safe. The app currently refuses to send any alert
because the boundary I have is an approximation, and it will keep refusing until
a real one is in place.

Specifically I am looking for:

- The Marikina River basin catchment polygon (or the sub-basin set covering
  Rodriguez / San Mateo / Marikina / Pasig)
- Any accompanying flood hazard layers (5/25/100-year return periods) already
  published for the area
- The attribution wording you would like used in-app

The app carries a prominent notice that it is **not** an official warning
service, tells users to follow PAGASA, OCD and their LGU regardless of what it
shows, and never displays a lower alert level than PAGASA. All data is
attributed to its publisher with a direct link.

If there is a licence, data-sharing agreement or attribution requirement I
should complete first, I am glad to follow it.

Thank you for your time and for making this data available at all.
